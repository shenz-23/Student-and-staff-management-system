About Our System

The Student and Staff Management System (SSMS) is designed to make university management easier and more efficient. It provides a central platform where students can access academic information, lecturers can manage teaching activities, and administrators can organize university operations.

What Can You Do With SSMS?

Everything you need to manage your academic activities.

🎓

Student Portal

View timetables, assignments, attendance, grades, and academic notifications.

📚

Lecturer Portal

Manage classes, study materials, assignments, student results, and feedback.

⚙️

Admin Portal

Manage student and lecturer accounts, courses, schedules, and university information.
